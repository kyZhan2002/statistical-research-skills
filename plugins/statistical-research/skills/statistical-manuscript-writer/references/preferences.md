# Compact style contract

- Source understanding precedes writing.
- Preserve current notation and local LaTeX conventions.
- Prefer rigorous, concise, natural statistical prose over decorative language.
- Explain context and interpretation, but do not pad.
- Never fabricate citations, results, assumptions, or novelty claims.
- Keep theorem statements minimal; put technical detail where readers expect it.
- Use inline math more often than unnecessary display equations.
- Do not use `\begin{paragraph}`; avoid unnecessary source-line breaks.
- Treat compile correctness, labels, and references as part of writing quality.
