# Simulation audit checklist

For each figure or table, verify:
- exact data-generating model;
- factor varied and values;
- quantities held fixed;
- estimator/tuning implementation;
- baselines and fairness of comparison;
- replication count and uncertainty display;
- metric definition and scale;
- whether cross-setting comparisons are valid;
- intended theoretical or reviewer question;
- conclusion supported by the actual plotted data.
